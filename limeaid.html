Who it's for

Built for athletes who feel every sound.

Crowds, whistles, echoing gyms. For many autistic athletes, the sensory load of a game can drown out the one voice that matters: the coach.

LimeAid pairs earbuds with two app modes. Athletes set how loud the world gets. Coaches pick an athlete and talk straight to them, clearly and privately.

What it does

Four controls. Nothing extra.

Every setting in the app maps to one clear job, on high-contrast dark screens with big targets.

Coach volume

Set how loud the coach's voice comes through, separately from everything else.

Ambient volume

Turn the crowd, the buzzer and the echo down to a level that feels manageable.

Voice focus

Make voices stand out more clearly from ambient sound with a single switch.

Haptics

A dial sets the strength of gentle vibration cues, for signals that don't need sound at all.
