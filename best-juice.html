About the rebrand

Heritage taste,
new energy.

This rebrand gives a heritage Egyptian juice a louder, friendlier voice. The B doubles as a juice box with a straw, and the Arabic wordmark carries the tagline "Best is the best."

Bilingual from the start: Coiny and Montserrat for Latin, a rounded display face and Noto Sans for Arabic, so both scripts feel like one family.
