The kit

Three pieces, one attitude. Every pack is black, every label is the same stamped lockup, and every product gets its own burst of hand-drawn lettering.

01 · Pencils

Colored Pencils

A tall black sleeve with a cut-out window, so the colors do the selling. The pink "PENCILS" burst tears up through the window line, and the box reads "9 Colored Pencils, for the restless."

Concept product
02 · Notebook

Notebook

Ships in a flat black mailer. The lid carries the "NOTEBOOK" burst in pink with blue speed lines, and an orange star wraps the corner.

03 · Carry

The Bag

Quiet on the front: just the lockup and "for the restless." The side gusset is where it gets loud, stacked with pink, blue, orange and red stars.

Sketch pad

Pick a pencil from the box and draw. Switch to the star stamp to slap stars around like the bag's gusset. Save your page when you're done.
