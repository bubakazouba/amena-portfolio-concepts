One square

Every word is folded into a single block, so the text reads as an image first and a sentence second.

02

Two inks

Navy carries the upper lines; red pools in the lower half, splitting the composition by color as well as by line.

03

Round ends

Strokes keep an even weight with softened terminals, and the dots sit on the same grid as the letters.
