Overview + The problem (sample #1)

Before

Sahl helps people manage all their household payments in one place. The goal was to turn a fragmented payment landscape into one seamless experience that saves time and reduces anxiety.
The problem: Inconsistent service flows and overwhelming navigation caused high drop-off and confusion. The existing system couldn’t scale with Sahl’s growing list of services.

After

Sahl lets people pay all their household bills in one app. Each of its 200+ services had been built a little differently, so people got lost, picked the wrong provider, or gave up on the first screen. My goal was to make every payment work the same way, so it's faster and people feel sure it went through.
